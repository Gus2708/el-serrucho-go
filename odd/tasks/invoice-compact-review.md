"en lo del precio también pon el costo que se detectó en la factura": show detected invoice unit cost adjacent to selling price, distinguishing net stock-unit cost when different and retaining non-USD guards.
- S3: "tambien revisa porque da tantos productos que no tienen que ver": reproduce the generator / fumigadora / voltage-protector example from the supplied screenshots through the real matcher. Use a general, conservative relevance rule rather than per-SKU exceptions. Preserve code/alias conflicts, synonym-only retrieval, manual catalog search and partial-result warnings. No perfect-catalog accuracy claim; disclose unproven retrieval-only identity and measured recall tradeoffs.
- S4: "el IVA y descuentos no los pongas en producto por producto ponlo para la factura completa"; answer to percentage-vs-fixed-USD question: "por porcentaje". Present IVA and percentage discount controls once for the invoice. Reuse existing persisted edits, explicit human authority, exact decimals, and atomic batch mutation. Sequential percentages apply before IVA; retain printed pre-discount line totals and net stock-unit costs without double-discount/double-IVA. Fixed-USD global allocation remains blocked. Existing mixed/exempt lines and already-included discounts are not silently overwritten; explicit changes remain possible. This supersedes older primary per-line IVA/discount presentation and blanket percentage-global-policy blocking only as needed for S1/S4, not other guards.
- S5: Local implementation only. Preserve quantities, units/conversion, literal codes, PVP confirmation, source/session/version fences, existing saved-revision JSON schema, recalculation/materialization authority, and imported-emission block. No DB migration, remote SQL, provider call, uploads, deployment, push, APK/EAS, credential reads or dependency install. Shared calculation changes require matching Edge deployment before hosted discounted-invoice use; that deployment needs separate authorization. Observe deterministic RED before source changes, preservation cases and baseline differential; independent verification for shared contracts/async ordering and one batched mobile/desktop visual round with at most one scoped correction/recheck.
- S6: "en el precio si el producto ya existia debe estar escrito el precio que ya tiene igual que en la vista de compras". On explicit existing-product selection, adopt valid current catalog precio_venta as the actual editable, persisted selling price in the same atomic selection edit; it already includes IVA. Never replace documentary quantity/cost with catalog values or multiply existing PVP by markup/IVA. Preserve a same-SKU manual override; a successful different-SKU choice must not carry the old product's price or invalid draft. New products alone retain existing cost-based suggestions. Saved associated products without persisted PVP must visibly distinguish an unapplied current catalog price and require explicit adoption before approval/materialization, not silently mutate saved/approved content. Displayed, saved and materialized prices must agree.
- S7: "el usuario no se los sabe de memoria debe haber un buscador de productos igual que en el buscar producto de compra donde se muestren los productos en inventario". Reuse a shared purchase-style full inventory picker with name/description, code or barcode search and current inventory results, not only RAG suggestions. Selection outside suggestions uses current manual association/motive authority. Loading, pagination, retry/error, empty, placeholders, permission denial and stale/cancelled results must remain distinct; failure/empty partial reads never prove newness.
- S8: "ademas hay muchos pasos que son muy tediosos" and "tambien hay muchas preguntas que puedes hacer en general en vez de estar repitiendolo una y otra vez". Consolidate invoice-wide document type, price inclusion, common IVA, optional discount and existing explicit unresolved-only factor-one batch. Present a compact answered summary and secondary exceptions rather than repeating primary questions on every product. Preserve mixed/exempt and package exceptions, per-product identity/PVP, manual motives and final receipt/document/save/approval/draft authority. Apply requested guidance from https://github.com/leonxlnx/taste-skill, https://github.com/mattpocock/skills, https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill and Impeccable to an adaptive Operate surface; no marketing hero/photos/shaders or global rebrand/installers. Keep the existing dark/gold theme, aligned hierarchy, clear labels,44px targets and measured mobile/desktop usability. Tap estimates are not empirical speed claims.
